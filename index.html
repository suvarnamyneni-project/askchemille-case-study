Focus of this study
Guided search: query, clarifying question, ranked results with % match, grade detail, quote or expert

Choosing an engineering polymer is a high-stakes call made early in product design. A wrong grade surfaces late, in validation or tooling, when change is expensive. Yet engineers usually start with a loose need ("heat-resistant, glass-filled, for a fuel cap"), a spec number, or a competitor's grade name, not a Celanese trade name.

The catalog is deep: the live search lists 2,115 grades and the resource library 448 documents, each grade carrying dense data on chemistry, filler, processing, chemical resistance and certifications. Keyword search and long filter panels push the translation work onto the user.

How might we

help a design engineer turn a plain-language need, or a competitor's grade, into a short, trustworthy list of Celanese grades, and know exactly when to bring in a human expert?

  • Users arrive from four starting points: alternatives, application, filters, or a live expert (as the site's FAQ lists them).
  • AI output must stay honest: the site labels chemical-resistance data as guidance only, to be validated.
  • Premium content (documents, quotes, chat) sits behind a free account, so the free path still has to deliver value.
  • Global audience in two languages, with trademark, GDPR and cookie-consent requirements.

03

End-users

Design engineers at OEMs and Tier-1 suppliers

Need a grade that meets thermal, mechanical and chemical demands for a new part; often start from an application or property.

Materials and application engineers

Validate shortlists against specs, OEM approvals, UL listings and chemical-resistance data; want data sheets and documents.

Engineers replacing a current material

Start from a competitor grade name and need a like-for-like Celanese alternative.

Sourcing and procurement specialists

Need quotes, availability and colours once engineering has a shortlist.

Sustainability-driven teams

Look for bio-content, carbon-capture or PFAS-alternative grades.

Industries offered as entry points on the site: Automotive and Transportation, Consumer Goods, Electrical and Electronics, Industrial, and Medical and Pharma.

Heuristic findings

Findings below are the evaluator's reading of those screens. They are not user quotes, and they are not counts of how often a problem occurs.

Nielsen heuristics against the 30 Oct 2026 desktop captures.
Heuristic What is observable What the concept does with it
Visibility of system status The results headline states a count, and parsed intent is a chip the person can remove. Keep the count and the chips. When a percent match is shown, also say which comparison it is.
Match with the real world Examples and the assistant use application language ("for extrusion", "similar to ultramid a3wg6"), not only trade names. Keep plain-language search as the front door, with "See all grades & filter" for people who already know the facets.
User control Skip sits with the answer chips. Filter chips can be removed. The grade page keeps the shortlist in a rail. Keep Skip and the rail. Do not force the clarifying question.
Consistency The same side nav, pill buttons, chips, and email/chat pair appear on home, results, alternatives, grade, and the gate. Keep that system. The UI kit on this page records the tokens already in the live CSS.
Error prevention Alternatives asks which Ultramid variant before it ranks. The full grade page labels chemical resistance as guidance. Keep the variant question and the guidance caveat. Do not present a percent as a specification.
Recognition rather than recall The first results screen asks one question as chips instead of opening the full filter set. Keep one question. Leave the batch filter modal for spec-driven searches.
Flexibility Sort and colour sit above the cards. Strong matches and partial matches are separate blocks, with an expert prompt after both. Keep the split and the expert prompt so a weak list still has a next step.
Minimal design Home is a headline, one field, and two actions. The account pitch on home is a card. The gate appears later, over results. Keep search and browse open. Open the gate on quote, documents, and the other locked actions, not on arrival.
Recover from a thin explanation The alternatives card leads with "98% match …, +1". The collapsed card does not list the properties that produced 98%. "+1" is the only sign that another comparison exists. This is the main change in the concept: the match label should name the basis, not only the percent and a competitor grade.
Help Home offers Quick guide and a playlist marked 31 videos. Email and chat are fixed on the captured screens. Keep both. The expert path stays available when the list is only a partial match.

Opportunity gaps

  • Databases expect users to speak in filters; none of the reviewed pages describe a search that asks the user a clarifying question.
  • Alternatives search is paywalled on UL Prospector and not described elsewhere; a free, explained % match is a clear differentiator.
  • Multi-supplier databases hand users off to suppliers; a supplier-owned tool can offer an engineer in the same session.
  • Sustainability and PFAS-alternative discovery is emerging as its own search intent.
  • Most tools gate advanced features by price; a free account gate tied to intent lowers the barrier.

09

Prototype

There is no separate prototype file. The concept is the two flows in Design Decisions, walked below against the live desktop screens from 30 Oct 2026. It was not tested with participants.

  1. Home: describe the need

    Type "PPS grade for extrusion" in the hero search, or pick an industry.

  2. AI parses the query

    PPS appears as a removable chip; the headline confirms 17 grades.

  3. Answer one clarifying question

    Choose Profile Extrusion from the processing chips, or Skip; the result count updates.

  4. Scan ranked results

    Strong matches first, partial matches below, and an expert prompt at the end.

  5. Open a grade

    Fortron® FX20T1: summary, key features, chemical resistance, properties, colours.

  6. Act

    Request quote or View Documents opens the free-account gate for guests; chat is always available.

  7. Alternate path

    Search a competitor grade, choose the variant, review % matches, compare.

The walkthrough uses the captured guest path: home, the clarifying question before a chip is chosen, strong matches and partial matches, a competitor-grade percent match, grade detail, the full grade page, and the account gate over results. Loading, zero-result, signed-in, and mobile states were not captured.

Final Designs

Annotated screens of the live site (desktop, 1440 px wide, captured 30 Oct 2026). They are the reference for the concept, not a claim that this study shipped them.

The captures below are the live desktop screens. Select one to enlarge it. Arrow keys move between images. Escape closes the viewer.

Home: the front door

  1. Two-tone hero, "Welcome to the Chemille® Digital Assistant, your gateway to endless materials knowledge", in Midnight Violet and Muted Lilac.
  2. Search field with rotating example prompts teaches the kind of query that works.
  3. Primary "See all grades & filter" for filter-first users; tonal "Quick guide" for newcomers.
  4. Right column: 31-video tutorial playlist and a free-account value card with six benefits.
  5. "Start searching by Industry" menu for users unsure where to begin.

Guided results: the clarifying question

  1. Query stays editable at the top, with a save-search action.
  2. Parsed intent becomes a filter chip (PPS ×) beside Filters and Add Filter.
  3. Assistant headline states the count, then asks one question: processing type for "extrusion".
  4. Answer chips (Film, Other, Profile, Sheet Extrusion) plus Skip keep control with the user.
  5. Sort and colour controls sit above cards; an in-list card invites a free account.

Alternatives: % match to a competitor grade

  1. Assistant confirms "11 grades similar to 'ultramid a3wg6'" and asks which variant.
  2. Variant chips disambiguate before ranking, preventing a wrong baseline.
  3. Results sorted by relevance; each card shows the match in orange, e.g. "98% match to Ultramid® A3WG6 LT BK, +1".

Grade detail: evidence and action

  1. Strong-match rail on the left keeps the shortlist one click away.
  2. Grade name, polymer and a one-sentence summary, then Request quote and View Documents.
  3. Jump-to anchors: Chemical Resistance, Properties, Available Grade Colors, Talk to a specialist.
  4. Chemical-resistance tables by chemical family, with a guidance-only disclaimer.
  5. Custom-colour prompt with Email Us and Chat With Us.

Account gate: value at the moment of intent

  1. Opens on gated actions, over the results the user was already viewing.
  2. Title "Sign in or create an account." with "free" in Electric Violet.
  3. Six benefits with green checks and lock icons show exactly what unlocks.

Outcome

What is publicly verifiable about the product, not about this study: Chemille launched on 28 April 2025, and as of 30 Oct 2026 the live product lists 2,115 grades, 448 resources and 31 tutorial videos in English and Chinese, and has added search types such as OEM specification, regulatory and certification, and PFAS-alternative discovery.

This study did not ship features, and it has no adoption, lead, or satisfaction numbers. The deliverable is the concept on this page.

Concept deliverables and the heuristic issues they respond to. Not measured outcomes.
Deliverable What the concept specifies
Information architecture and two flows A sitemap, a plain-language path from query to quote or expert, and a competitor-grade path that disambiguates the variant before it ranks.
Explained match Addresses the heuristic gap on the alternatives card: "98% match to Ultramid® A3WG6 LT BK, +1" is visible, and the properties behind the percent are not. The concept keeps the orange label and asks it to name the basis of the score.
One optional clarifying question Keeps the single processing question and Skip. The captured screen still shows 17 grades before a chip is chosen, so Skip must remain a real choice rather than a dead end.
Action before the long page The full grade capture continues through chemical resistance, properties, and colours. The concept keeps Request quote, View Documents, and the jump links above that evidence, and keeps the guidance-only caveat.
Gate at the moment of intent Home can be used without an account. The gate in the capture opens over results and lists six locked benefits. The concept keeps that placement.
Validate next A moderated task test of the two flows with design engineers, plus a check of whether an explained match is trusted more than the bare percent. No such test has been run. The targets in Metrics are the success criteria for that future test.
